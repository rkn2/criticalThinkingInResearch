Each round, 20 minutes

MinBlockWhat happens
0–5ReadThe presenter passes around their storymap worksheets: the “Whole U” page and the slide plan. Listeners read silently and write two questions on the feedback card. The presenter sets up the deck.
5–10PitchThe presenter gives the 5-minute pitch. No interruptions. The timekeeper stops it at 5:00.
10–15QuestionsListeners ask committee-style questions: start with the two you wrote, then whatever the pitch raised. The presenter answers in 30 seconds or less, then stops. Anyone can ask a follow-up, any time.
15–20FeedbackListeners go through the card stop by stop, the answers included. The presenter writes the feedback on the last page of the packet, and the listeners hand over their cards.
AskingAsk what a committee member would ask, one thing at a time. Ask why and what if, not just what. Always follow up if an answer was long, vague or missed: “So is that a yes or a no?” Anyone can follow up, not just the person who asked.
AnsweringShort and to the point. One sentence that answers it, one sentence of why, then stop. If they want more, they will ask. Don’t know? “I don’t know yet; here’s how I’d find out.” Answer the question, don’t defend the slide.

Results: say where you are Either is fine. A Result slide that only lists what you did is not.

Have resultsShow them, and say which hypothesis they favor. Say what it means, not what you ran.
Partial or none yetShow what you have, and label the rest EXPECTED: what you think you will find, and which hypothesis that would support.
