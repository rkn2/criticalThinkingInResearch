Critical Thinking in Research Syllabus Readings Resources

Readings

Week 1
Heilmeier's Catechism
DARPA, 1 page. Forces you to answer "what are you doing and why" before anything else.
Week 2
Booth et al., The Craft of Research — 3.4.1–3.4.3
4th ed., pp. 43–46. The three-step formula: Topic → Question → Significance.
Week 2 → 3
Platt, "Strong Inference"
Science, 1964, ~8 pp. The original case for hypothesis-driven research.
Week 3 → 4
Feynman, "Cargo Cult Science"
1974 Caltech commencement, ~5 pp. The difference between doing science and imitating it.
Week 4 → 5
Whitesides, "Writing a Paper"
Advanced Materials, 2004, ~4 pp. Building a paper from an outline, not accumulated text.
Weeks 5–6
Research design chapter (Becca assigns)
Experimental design principles.
Week 7
Chapter from "How to Write an Impactful Research Paper"
Connecting validation to the written argument.
Week 10
Scientific graphics book (Becca assigns)
Making results visible.
